# Problem Description and Solution

## Problem Statement

The Airbnb market is growing rapidly and is increasingly competitive.  
**Hosts** want to know how to set the right price and make their listings attractive, while **travelers** struggle to compare offers objectively.  

### Identified Issues
- Lack of visibility on **average prices** in a given region.  
- Difficulty in **objectively evaluating a listing** (price, amenities, location, reviews).  
- Airbnb data is **scattered** and not easily accessible in an exploitable form.  

---

## Proposed Solution: SmartBnB

We developed **SmartBnB**, a platform that centralizes and analyzes Airbnb data to make it accessible through interactive visualizations and scoring.

### Key Features
- **Listing evaluation**: given an Airbnb link, calculation of an objective score based on:  
  - price compared to market,  
  - available amenities,  
  - location,  
  - reviews and ratings.  
- **Interactive heatmap**: visualization of average prices by region or neighborhood.  
- **Top 10 rankings**: dynamic lists (best ratings, lowest prices, most booked).  
- **Price evolution**: chart showing price trends over the past year.

### Benefits
- For **travelers**: quickly compare listings and find the best value for money.  
- For **hosts**: receive objective feedback on their listing and adjust pricing or services.
